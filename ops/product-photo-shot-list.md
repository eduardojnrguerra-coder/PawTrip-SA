# PawTrip SA Product Photo Shot List

Use this shot list for hero products, bundles and best sellers. Aim for honest, accurate product representation. Do not use AI images for exact product representation unless the image accurately reflects the real product.

## Hero Product Shot List

For each hero product:

- [ ] Product on white/clean background
- [ ] In-car photo
- [ ] Close-up material
- [ ] Close-up straps/clips
- [ ] Size/measurement photo
- [ ] Lifestyle dog photo
- [ ] Before/after car protection

## Recommended Product Groups

Start with:

- Road Trip Starter Kit

- Foldable Dog Ramp for Cars and SUVs

## Photo Notes

- Keep lighting natural and clean.
- Show scale clearly where size matters.
- Photograph straps, buckles, texture and seams.
- Avoid exaggerated before/after results.
- Keep dogs comfortable and supervised during lifestyle shots.
