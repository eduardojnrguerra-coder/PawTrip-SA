# PawTrip SA Product Sourcing Research

Private operations document. Do not publish supplier names, supplier costs, wholesale notes or sourcing assumptions on the public PawTrip SA website.

Last updated: 2026-05-08

## Evaluation Rules

- Prefer local South African suppliers.
- Prefer suppliers with clear stock availability or trade/wholesale access.
- Prefer products that are not extremely bulky.
- Avoid products with medical claims.
- Avoid pet food/treat products unless legal registration, labelling and ingredient details are confirmed.
- Avoid copying competitor photos without permission.
- Avoid products where courier cost destroys margin.

## Decision Notes

### Best early-list candidates

- Pet Seat Belt Clip
- Collapsible Dog Travel Bowl
- Pet Hair Removal Brush
- Dog Drying Towel
- Slow Feeder Bowl
- Reflective Dog Leash
- Waterproof Dog Car Seat Cover if fit/material specs are confirmed

### Wait until confirmed

- SUV Dog Boot Liner: bulky courier cost could damage margin.
- Foldable Dog Ramp: high courier/volumetric risk; prefer direct shipping from supplier.
- Training Treats and Dental Chews: require legal labelling/registration, ingredient and expiry details before listing.
- Adjustable Dog Harness: sizing returns risk; list only with strong size guide and exchange policy.

### Photo and content rules

- Ask permission before using supplier photos or specifications.
- Do not copy photos from competitor sites.
- If supplier photos are not approved, buy samples and photograph hero products.
- Avoid medical claims such as preventing arthritis, curing anxiety, improving digestion or treating dental disease unless supported by compliant product documentation.

## Outreach Priority

1. Daro Pet Products: broad wholesale catalogue for accessories, toys, bowls, grooming, leads and harnesses.
2. Akwa: wholesale-only pet accessories lead; request login/trade pricing.
3. Kunduchi / Stone Hill / Red Dog Dezign: car protection and locally made textile products.
4. Pet Brands / Boutique Pet Supplies / Petsa: enrichment, bowls, toys and premium accessory brands.
5. Rogz / Meelo Pets: walking gear with local brand credibility.
6. Good Dawg / Complete Pet Supplies: treats only after legal/labelling checks.
7. Ramp suppliers: only proceed if supplier can direct ship or courier cost leaves enough margin.
